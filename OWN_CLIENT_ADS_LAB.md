# Own-Client Ads Lab

## Principle

Do not sell an advertising capability before we have used the same workflow on an owned product and measured it.

The purpose is not to claim one small campaign proves expert status. The purpose is to build:
- creative production skill;
- ad testing discipline;
- landing-page/offer skill;
- tracking discipline;
- a repeatable SOP;
- truthful portfolio evidence.

## First eligible owned assets

Use whichever has a live purchase/checkout path first:
1. a consumer product/book with a real checkout;
2. STR Turnover Proof System once its marketplace listing is live;
3. future digital marketplace products.

Free tools such as TurnoverCue can be used for CTR/lead/usage testing, but not as proof of profitable paid acquisition until a money event exists.

## Creative test system

For each product, create 3 distinct ad concepts rather than 3 cosmetic variants.

Concept A — Problem / pain
- show the frustration or cost of the current workaround;
- product appears as the resolution.

Concept B — Demonstration
- show the product being used;
- rapid before → process → after.

Concept C — Outcome / identity
- show the result the buyer wants or the person they want to be.

Each concept gets:
- 1 short vertical reel/video;
- 1 static image;
- 2 hooks;
- 2 primary-text variants.

Do not test 20 variables simultaneously.

## Metrics

Top of funnel:
- thumb-stop / 3-second view rate where available;
- CTR;
- CPC.

Commercial:
- landing-page view;
- add-to-cart / checkout;
- purchase;
- cost per purchase;
- revenue / ad spend.

If selling through a marketplace that does not expose full conversion tracking, log:
- clicks/outbound clicks;
- marketplace views;
- favourites/cart events where available;
- sales during the test window.

## Budget ladder

Phase 0: organic creative QA — A$0.
Phase 1: A$20–30 total to reject obviously weak creative.
Phase 2: A$50–100 only if Phase 1 produces encouraging click/engagement behaviour and a functioning checkout.
Phase 3: scale only after purchase evidence.

Never scale a campaign merely because it gets cheap clicks.

## Graduation to a sellable service

We do not market ourselves as an ad-performance expert from one campaign.

We may sell a narrow service such as:
- "3 Meta Reel concepts + hooks for your product"
- "ad creative test pack"
- "landing-page + ad creative audit"

after we can show:
- actual creatives;
- actual campaign metrics;
- the workflow used;
- what we learned and changed.

Higher-value performance/lead-gen services require stronger proof.

## Output from every campaign

1. Creative pack.
2. Campaign settings log.
3. Results sheet.
4. What worked / failed.
5. Next test.
6. Reusable SOP.

The skill becomes an owned operational asset.

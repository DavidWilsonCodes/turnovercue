QUICK GUIDE

Questions before you try it?

What file do I upload?

TurnoverCue uses standard .ics or iCalendar files. Airbnb, VRBO and many other booking systems allow hosts to export or share calendars in this format.

Where do I get the Airbnb calendar file?

In Airbnb, open the listing calendar and look for calendar availability or calendar sync/export options. Airbnb can provide an iCal link or calendar file representing the listing’s bookings and unavailable dates.

Can I upload more than one property?

Yes. Upload multiple .ics files and TurnoverCue combines them into one turnover schedule. The filename is used as the property name.

Does TurnoverCue see my prices or payouts?

No. TurnoverCue does not connect to your Airbnb or VRBO account and does not need access to financial information.

Does my booking data get uploaded?

No. The current free version processes the calendar locally in your browser.

What is a same-day turnover?

A same-day turnover happens when one guest checks out and the next guest checks in on the same date. TurnoverCue highlights these because they usually require tighter cleaner coordination.

Will it work with every booking platform?

TurnoverCue can read standard iCalendar files, but platforms format them differently. Airbnb-specific blocked and unavailable events are filtered where identifiable. Other feeds are currently treated as beta support.

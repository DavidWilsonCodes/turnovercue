THE JOB

Built for the boring 10 minutes after export

When a campaign has dozens of variants, naming and ordering the files can take longer than it deserves — especially when the naming convention has to survive handoff to a media buyer or client.

1️⃣

Drop the whole export batch

Keep the original files untouched and work on copies in the browser.

2️⃣

Define the naming rule once

Use campaign, concept, platform, original filename, date and an automatic sequence number.

3️⃣

Ship one clean handoff

Download renamed files in one ZIP and a manifest that maps old names to new names.

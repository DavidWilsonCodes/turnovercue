The gap TurnoverCue removes

Airbnb already knows when a guest checks out. Your cleaner usually only needs the operational version of that information: which property needs cleaning, on what date, and whether another guest arrives the same day.

TurnoverCue takes an exported iCalendar file and turns those reservation dates into a simple turnover schedule. It can combine multiple property files, highlight same-day turns, create a cleaner-ready message, and export a CSV or printable schedule.

How it works

1. Get your Airbnb calendarAirbnb provides an iCal calendar link from the listing calendar's availability/connect-calendar settings.
2. Upload the .ics fileTurnoverCue processes the calendar locally in your browser.
3. Send the cleaner scheduleCopy the message, export CSV, or print/save the schedule.

Why not just share the whole Airbnb account?

A cleaner generally does not need access to pricing, payouts, guest messaging or the rest of the host dashboard. TurnoverCue is deliberately narrow: it turns booking timing into cleaning timing.

Where to export an Airbnb calendar

Airbnb's current host help says that on desktop you can open Calendar → select the listing → Availability → Connect calendars → Connect to another website, then copy the exported calendar link. Airbnb notes that iCal links end in .ics.

Airbnb occasionally changes interface labels, so use its current Help Center if your screen differs.

Frequently asked questions

Is TurnoverCue an Airbnb integration?

No. It does not sign in to Airbnb or use your Airbnb account credentials. You provide an exported .ics calendar file.

Can I use multiple Airbnb properties?

Yes. Upload multiple calendar files and TurnoverCue combines their turnover dates into one schedule.

Does it flag same-day turnovers?

Yes. If the next reservation starts on the checkout date, TurnoverCue highlights the turnover as same-day.

Does my booking calendar get uploaded?

The free version processes the calendar locally in your browser rather than uploading the booking file to a TurnoverCue server.
